A strong Business Analyst CV makes your value easy to understand. Recruiters should quickly see the problems you can help solve, the stakeholders you can work with, and the evidence that you can support change.

## Start with a clear profile

Your profile should explain the direction you are moving in, the transferable strengths you already have, and the practical BA skills you are building.

## Translate your experience

Many learners already have useful examples from customer service, operations, admin, compliance, finance, healthcare, education, or project support. The key is to describe them through Business Analysis language.

## Show BA evidence

Use clear examples of requirements, process improvement, stakeholder communication, documentation, analysis, problem solving, and collaboration.

## Keep it recruiter-friendly

Use simple headings, clear dates, strong bullet points, and language that matches the UK Business Analyst market.
