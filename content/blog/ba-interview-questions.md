Business Analyst interviews test more than definitions. Employers want to know how you think, how you communicate, and how you would handle real workplace situations.

## Expect role questions

You may be asked what a Business Analyst does, why you want the role, and how your background connects to Business Analysis.

## Prepare stakeholder examples

Interviewers often want to hear how you manage expectations, ask questions, clarify confusion, and communicate with different people.

## Practise requirements scenarios

Be ready to explain how you gather requirements, document them, validate them, and manage changes.

## Show your learning mindset

If you are new to Business Analysis, honesty matters. Show what you have practised, how you think, and how you keep improving.

## Build confidence before interviews

Mock interviews, feedback, and clear BA language can help you enter interviews with more confidence and structure.
