Terms and conditions

Plain-English terms for Anderseed Consulting resources, mentorship applications, payment, refunds, and programme participation.

Last updated: August 2026. This page is a practical working draft and should be professionally reviewed before launch.

Services

Anderseed Consulting provides mentorship and career education relating to Business Analysis. Services may include live and recorded sessions, guided project work, portfolio development, resources, CV and LinkedIn support, interview preparation, community access, and career guidance.

No employment guarantee

We do not guarantee employment, interviews, salary outcomes, or completion within a particular timeframe. The 90-day and six-month journey shown on the website is an illustrative happy path. Outcomes depend on your effort, prior experience, location, market conditions, applications, interview performance, right-to-work position, and hiring decisions outside our control.

Enrolment

Cohort places are confirmed after payment or an approved payment arrangement. Cohorts are kept small and places may be limited to preserve collaboration, learner support, and feedback quality.

Payments

Payment options may include card payment, Klarna where available, or bank transfer. Bank transfer references should include your full name and BA Mentorship so payments can be matched correctly.

Refunds

You have a 14-day cooling-off period from purchase under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. If you ask us to begin providing sessions, access, or personalised services during that period and then cancel, a proportionate amount may be deducted for services already supplied.

After the 14-day cancellation period, programme fees are generally non-refundable except where required by law or your statutory rights. Individual services such as CV writing, CV review, interview preparation, and LinkedIn review are non-refundable once delivered.

Transfers and programme changes

One transfer to a future cohort may be allowed when requested before the original programme starts, subject to available places and written confirmation. If Anderseed cancels a cohort and cannot provide a suitable replacement, affected learners may choose a full refund for the undelivered programme or transfer to an available future cohort.

Sessions and recordings

Teaching sessions are recorded for enrolled learners, normally with access for 12 months. Cameras are optional. Learners must avoid sharing unnecessary personal or confidential information during recorded sessions and must not copy, publish, forward, resell, or share recordings outside the authorised cohort.

Materials

Programme recordings, templates, resources, and community materials are for personal learning only. They may not be copied, shared, resold, published, or used to create competing services. Learners may use their own completed project work as a portfolio case study while protecting personal information, other learners' work, and restricted materials.

Conduct

Learners are expected to behave respectfully in sessions and community spaces. We may remove access where behaviour is abusive, disruptive, or harmful to other learners.

Your data

Personal data is handled in line with the privacy notice.
