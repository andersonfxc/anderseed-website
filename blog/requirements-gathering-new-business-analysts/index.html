Requirements gathering explained for new Business Analysts

How Business Analysts turn stakeholder conversations into clear, usable requirements.

Get Free BA RoadmapView mentorship

Requirements gathering is the process of understanding what people need, why they need it, and what a successful outcome should look like.

It starts with the problem

Before writing requirements, a Business Analyst should understand the business problem, current process, pain points, users, constraints, and expected outcome.

Useful questions to ask

  • What problem are we trying to solve?
  • Who is affected by this process or system?
  • What happens today, and where does it break down?
  • What does success look like?
  • What rules, risks, or dependencies should we know?

Requirements need structure

Good requirements are clear, testable, and connected to a business need. They may appear as user stories, acceptance criteria, process notes, business rules, or functional requirements.

Practice matters

New BAs build confidence by practising real scenarios: asking better questions, mapping processes, writing documentation, and receiving feedback.
