What recruiters look for in a Business Analyst CV

A practical guide to presenting transferable experience, BA skills, tools, and project examples with confidence.

Get Free BA RoadmapView mentorship

A strong Business Analyst CV does not simply list duties. It shows that you can understand problems, work with stakeholders, document requirements, and help teams move toward clear outcomes.

Recruiters scan for relevance quickly

Your profile, skills, and recent experience should make it obvious that you understand Business Analysis. Avoid vague statements and focus on evidence.

What to include

  • Requirements gathering, analysis, and documentation examples.
  • Stakeholder engagement and communication evidence.
  • Process improvement or problem-solving examples.
  • Tools such as Jira, Confluence, Excel, Visio, Miro, or similar platforms where relevant.
  • Project-style achievements, even if they came from non-BA roles.

Translate your old experience

If you have worked with customers, teams, reports, processes, systems, or operational problems, you may already have useful BA evidence. The CV needs to make that clear.

Make it ATS-friendly

Use clear headings, simple formatting, and relevant keywords. Avoid over-designed layouts that are hard for recruiters and systems to read.
